Jeffey The Mushroom Whisperer

This is a funky short story I wrote years ago with input from my younger brother With awful language and writing skills. We were trying to think of the randomest thing and try to make it funny and/or epic.
Uploaded May 25th, 2026

In the middle of the bottom of a great big valley sat a little log cabin. Inside this cabin lived a guy named Jeffey. He was a pretty normal person, but he had a secret power… Jeffey can hear the thoughts of mushrooms.

He would walk over the hills and up the mountains and listen to the little mushrooms and toadstools on the forest floor. At every base of every tree lived tens and hundreds of them. He would not try to talk back, and he would not bother them. He would simply listen. “Yummy, I love that warm sunlight!” he heard. “Oh no please don’t step on me!” squeaked another. Jeffey loved hearing them. They saw the world from a different angle.

Until one day in the midst of a storm, in all the rain and wind and thunder, there was lightning. Jeffey watched from the safety of his window as a massive crack of lightning zapped the mountainside. It lit up the night with flames battling against the rain. There was nothing he could do. The new danger was miles away. Jeffey went to sleep, hoping that the storm would be over by morning.

As the sun rose, Jeffey did along with it. Most of the mountain was still ablaze, and many of the trees were reduced to blackened poles. The mountain resembled a porcupine, if a porcupine could be half on fire and stand perfectly still as if nothing was wrong. Jeffey didn’t go back to the woods for a few days, he would rather not be on fire, thank you.

Mother nature has her ways. She likes to bring life to what once was, and so she did. Jeffey thought all the poor little mushrooms would surely be gone, but as he trekked carefully through the remains of the forest, he heard something unexpected.

“We have risen from the ashes of our ancestors!”

“Oh, yuck, it’s so dry and ashy around here. I hope it rains soon.”

“I wonder why those big creatures are picking us and throwing us into that thing?”

“No nope NO WAY don’t take me NOOO- wait this is fine, it’s kind of cozy in here… Huh.”

As his mind flooded with the tiny voices, he looked around his feet and discovered that there were indeed new fungi growing! There was so much chatter to take in at once, he almost didn’t catch the chaos happening around him. A small group of people were walking by him with bags and buckets full off mushrooms. They didn’t sound alarmed though, the shrooms seemed more than happy to be collected for eating.

That’s when he made his plan. Jeffey filled his shirt like a kangaroo pouch and lined his pockets with as many of the brown delicacies as he could carry, and off he went to sell them in town to the local restaurants. It was a nice payday, and so he got a bit too greedy. That evening, with only an hour of sunlight left, the mushroom whisperer hiked back up to the colonies of little dollar signs. He began to gather more, filling the bucket he brought along. Picking the place clean, he kept moving towards the area of the mountain the flames had not affected. Soon he would be running out of daylight. Until he heard a new train of thought.

“Hey you! Big slinky creature with the furry bit on top!” Jeffey stood still, trying to pinpoint the source of this very descriptive voice.

“Over here! The blue glowing one!”

In the dwindling light, he saw a mushroom that stood a bit bigger than the brown and white and gray varieties that surrounded it. It gave off a soft blue glow, and had ivory spots over the top. Jeffey crouched down to eye it carefully. “Is this some kind of trick?”

“Someone wants to meet you. Please follow us.” it thought back. A pathway of ocean colored shrooms led him through the trees and around the rocks, until he was met with quite the sight.

A wondrous array of glowing fungi dominated the clearing in the trees. You could feel the nicest breeze flow through the pines and over the grasses. A small creek trickled down from the peak of the mountain and cut through the scene, flowing out of sight. Fireflies danced in the air and frogs burbled. In the center of it all, stood the biggest mushroom Jeffey had ever laid eyes on. She stood twice his height, and was as wide as the length of his pickup truck. The beauty had the same glow as her little companions, and her spots were as big as Jeffey’s head. “It’s about time I got to meet the fabled Mushroom Whisperer.”

He stood in awe for quite some time. He couldn’t mutter much else than “Wow.” Jeffey noted that there was silence all around him. The only thoughts to be heard were hers. “I am Queen of the mushrooms. Lord of the toadstools. An agent of Mother Nature herself.” her voice was steady and firm. It was commanding in tone but sweet at the same time.

Jeffey was unsure how to carry himself. Should he bow? Run away? Is this a weird dream? Maybe he’s been hallucinating all these years and mushrooms don’t really have thoughts. Do they even have brains, he wondered? He just decided to stand there and look completely dumbfounded. “It’s an honor to meet you, your highness.” He shifted nervously.

The Queen looked as if she were shuddering in a way that was like a human rolling their eyes in annoyance. “I have called upon you to ask what you did today. I received word that you were picking mushrooms. Hundreds of them. For what reason?”

It felt like he should just run away, back to his safe and warm house where there are no talking fungi or plants or anything alive besides flies and spiders. His body told him to run, but his curiosity told him to stay. What could they do to him? They’re all stuck in the dirt they grew from.

“Well, I saw other people picking them to sell in town, I heard they make good money for doing it. None of them feel pain, so there’s nothing wrong with that. Right?” Maybe he should have lied. Maybe she would have known had he done so.

“It’s clear that you feel nothing is wrong with profiting from Mother Nature’s gifts, is that so?” and the queen’s glow turned to a deep purple. Then she turned red. All of her little subjects followed her example.

“What is happening? I didn’t mean any harm!” Jeffey gasped. “I respect everything around me! They were more than happy to be all cozied up in piles, you can ask them!” he pleaded, sensing that somehow, he was in danger. “I am the QUEEN. My power flows through this forest! I need my minions IN TACT so I may hear and see everything! You have disrupted ORDER! MY LEGACY WILL NOT BE TAINTED BY THE LIKES OF YOU!”

Jeffey could not run. It all happened too fast. From every mushroom in the clearing, puffy white spores arose from out of thin air. They shot towards the Mushroom Whisperer, swirling in a vortex around him. You could hear nothing but the screeching of wind and thunder. In the blink of an eye, they dispersed, and all that was left… were footprints.
